Clean Speaker - Water Eject

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-07

In short: no account, no server, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Tones are generated on your phone, the optional microphone check is deleted right after it plays back, and we do not collect any data from the app.

Who we are

Clean Speaker - Water Eject (“the app”) is made by Swapnil Jain (“we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: business@firstimpresslabs.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Your cleaning settings (mode, length, custom tone, vibration)
  • How many free cleanings you have used today (kept in your phone's keychain)
  • Your purchase status

Every tone is generated by the app on your phone and played through its own speaker. The app has no server and sends nothing to us.

What the app asks to use

Microphone. The app uses the microphone only for the optional microphone check, when you start it: it records 3 seconds and plays them straight back so you can hear how your microphone sounds. The recording is a temporary file on your phone and is deleted right after it plays back. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, payment card details, browsing or search history, usage analytics or crash data. We do not ask for or use the advertising identifier.

Who processes it

  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card details.

Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Your choices

  • Allow or remove access to the microphone in the Settings app at any time.

Everything the app keeps is deleted when you delete the app, except the free-cleaning counter in the keychain, which iOS may keep until you erase the phone. It holds only a date and a count.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. A change that collects more data will also be described in the app's release notes.
