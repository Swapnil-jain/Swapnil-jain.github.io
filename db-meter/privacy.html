dB Meter

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-14

In short: no account, no ads, no analytics and no server. The microphone only measures how loud it is, on your phone: nothing is recorded or sent. Your readings and settings stay on your phone.

Who we are

dB Meter (“the app”) is made by Swapnil Jain (“we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: business@firstimpresslabs.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Readings you save (sound levels, the time and the label you type)
  • Your settings, such as calibration and the alert level
  • Your answers to the onboarding questions
  • Your purchase status and saved-readings count (the count is kept in the iOS keychain, which can outlast deleting the app)

The home screen widget and the Siri shortcut show your last reading, which the app shares with them on your phone. If you ask Siri, Apple handles that request. Readings you export as CSV or PDF go only where you choose to share them.

What the app asks to use

Microphone. The app uses the microphone to measure sound levels while the meter is on. Sound is measured live and is never recorded or saved, and no audio leaves your phone.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, payment card details, browsing or search history, usage analytics or crash data. We do not ask for or use the advertising identifier.

Who processes it

  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card details.

Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Your choices

  • Allow or remove access to the microphone in the Settings app at any time.

You can delete saved readings in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. A change that collects more data will also be described in the app's release notes.
