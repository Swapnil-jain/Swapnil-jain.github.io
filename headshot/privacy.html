Pro Headshot: AI Photoshoot

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-14

In short: no account, no ads, no tracking. Only after you allow it, each photo you turn into a headshot goes through our server to Replicate and on to Google, which makes the AI model. We do not keep it. Your headshots stay on your phone.

Who we are

Pro Headshot: AI Photoshoot (“the app”) is made by Swapnil Jain (“we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: business@firstimpresslabs.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored on your device and are not sent to us:

  • The headshots you made, in your History
  • Your credit balance, the credits you have used and your subscription status (kept in your phone's keychain)
  • Whether you allowed photos to be sent for AI processing, and your settings
  • How many photos the AI service refused in the last day

The app does not keep the original photos you pick or take; it keeps only the headshots it made.

What the app asks to use

Camera. The app uses the camera to take photos to turn into headshots, up to five, only when you choose to. Those photos are sent for AI processing as described below, like photos you pick.

Photos. The app makes headshots from photos you pick (up to five at once), and saves results to your library only when you tap Save. When you pick a photo, Apple's photo picker gives the app only the photos you choose. The app cannot see the rest of your library. To save an image, the app asks only for permission to add photos to your library, which does not let it see your photos.

What we do not collect

Apart from what the AI feature sends, as described below, we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, payment card details, browsing or search history, usage analytics or crash data. We do not ask for or use the advertising identifier.

Photos you send for AI processing

Only after you allow it in the app, and only when you start making a headshot, the app sends each photo you picked or took (as a JPEG, made smaller if it is large), the gender you chose (male or female) and the shape you chose to our server, hosted on Cloudflare, which passes it to Replicate and Google to produce the result.

  • Replicate: the AI service our server sends the photo to. It passes the photo to Google. (privacy policy)
  • Google: makes and runs the AI model that turns the photo into a headshot. Replicate sends the photo to Google's own service for this, with our written instructions describing a business headshot. (privacy policy)

The headshot is downloaded to your phone from the address the AI service returns.

Our server passes the request to Replicate and the result back to the app. It does not keep your photos or the results, and it does not write them to its logs. Like any web host, Cloudflare sees the IP address of each request.

Replicate and Google may keep the photo and the result for a limited time under their own terms, linked above. We do not control how long they keep them.

If you tap Not now, nothing is sent, and the app asks again the next time you start a headshot.

The AI service may refuse a photo it flags as inappropriate. A refused photo uses no credit. After 3 refusals in a day, the app pauses headshots for 30 minutes.

To keep the service from being misused, the app uses Apple's App Attest to show our server that a request comes from the real app. For that, our server keeps a key Apple creates for the app on your phone. It is not linked to you and is used for nothing else.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare hosts the server that passes your photo to the AI provider, as our processor.
  • Replicate processes the photos you send, as described above.
  • Google processes the photos you send, as described above.
  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card details.

Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Your choices

  • Allow or remove access to the camera and your photos in the Settings app at any time.

You can delete History headshots one by one in the app. Everything the app keeps is deleted when you delete the app, except your credit balance and subscription status in the keychain, which iOS may keep until you erase the phone, so your credits are still there if you reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. A change that collects more data will also be described in the app's release notes.
