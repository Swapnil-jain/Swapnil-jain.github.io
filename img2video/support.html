Questions

How many videos do I get?

Your first AI video is free. Images to Video Pro gives 10 videos a week on the weekly plan and 3 a week on the yearly plan, counted over the last seven days. Every video is 5 seconds long.

What if a video fails?

A video that fails, that the AI's safety check refuses, or that you cancel is never counted. The app tells you on the spot.

Where does my photo go?

Only after you allow it, and only when you tap Animate, the photo and the motion you chose are sent to Replicate, which runs ByteDance's Seedance video model to make your video. Replicate deletes it about an hour later. You can turn this off in the app's Settings; then nothing is sent. Slideshows never leave your phone.

Why was my photo refused?

Photos of children, nudity or violence can't be animated, and the AI service runs its own safety check. A refused photo is not counted.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Images to Video AI, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Images to Video Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I reinstalled the app or got a new phone. How do I get Images to Video Pro back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings or on the Pro screen.
