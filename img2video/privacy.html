Images to Video AI

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-07

In short: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. A photo leaves your phone only when you allow AI processing and tap Animate; it goes to Replicate and ByteDance to make your video, Replicate deletes it about an hour later, and we do not keep it.

Who we are

Images to Video AI (“the app”) is made by Swapnil Jain (“we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: business@firstimpresslabs.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Your answers to the onboarding question and your settings
  • Whether you allowed photos to be sent for AI processing
  • The photos you animated, the videos and slideshows you made
  • How many free and weekly videos and slideshows you have used (kept in your phone's keychain)
  • Your purchase status

Slideshows are made entirely on your phone and are never sent anywhere. Your videos are kept in the app on your phone until you delete them; the app has no server copy of them.

What the app asks to use

Photos. You pick photos with the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you choose. When you tap Save to Photos, the app asks for permission to add the video to your library. When you pick a photo, Apple's photo picker gives the app only the photos you choose. The app cannot see the rest of your library. To save an image, the app asks only for permission to add photos to your library, which does not let it see your photos.

Notifications. If you allow it, the app shows a notification on this iPhone when a video you are making is ready or did not finish. It is scheduled on your phone; nothing about it is sent to us. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing about them is sent to us.

What we do not collect

Apart from what the AI feature sends, as described below, we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, payment card details, browsing or search history, usage analytics or crash data. We do not ask for or use the advertising identifier.

Photos you send for AI processing

Only after you allow it in the app, and only when you tap Animate, the app sends the photo you picked (made smaller first, at most 1600 pixels on its long side, JPEG, with no camera details or other metadata) and the motion you chose, as a short text description (a preset's description, or the words you typed) to our server, hosted on Cloudflare, which passes it to Replicate and ByteDance to produce the result.

  • Replicate: the AI service that runs the video model and returns the finished video. (privacy policy)
  • ByteDance: makes the video: Replicate runs ByteDance's Seedance video model through ByteDance's own service, so ByteDance receives the photo and the motion description to create the video. (privacy policy)

The finished video is downloaded to your phone from the address the AI service returns, and kept only on your phone.

Our server passes the request to Replicate and the result back to the app. We do not keep a copy of your photos, your descriptions or the videos. Like any web host, Cloudflare sees the IP address of each request. Requests carry the app's name and version, never anything that identifies you.

Replicate states that the inputs, outputs and logs of predictions made through its API are deleted automatically about an hour after the prediction finishes. ByteDance processes the request to create the video under its own privacy policy.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare hosts the server that passes your photo to the AI provider, as our processor.
  • Replicate processes the photos you send, as described above.
  • ByteDance processes the photos you send, as described above.
  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card details.

Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Your choices

  • Allow or remove access to your photos in the Settings app at any time.
  • Turn off notifications for the app in the Settings app.

Delete a video or slideshow in My Videos. Everything the app keeps is deleted when you delete the app, except the usage counter in the keychain, which iOS may keep until you erase the phone. It holds only dates and counts.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. A change that collects more data will also be described in the app's release notes.
