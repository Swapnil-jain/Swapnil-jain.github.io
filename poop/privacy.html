Poop Tracker - Toilet Log

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-07

In short: no account, no server, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything you log stays on your phone. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. We do not collect any data from the app.

Who we are

Poop Tracker - Toilet Log (“the app”) is made by Swapnil Jain (“we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: business@firstimpresslabs.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored on your device and are not sent to us:

  • The bowel movements you log: time, Bristol type, color, effort, pain, the things you note (such as blood or urgency) and your notes
  • What you log alongside them: water, fiber, food, medicines and symptoms
  • The name and reason for tracking you choose to print on doctor reports
  • Your reminder times and settings
  • Your purchase status

A doctor report, spreadsheet or backup file you create goes only where you choose to send it from the share sheet. The app does not upload it anywhere.

What the app asks to use

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the app sends a daily check-in reminder at the time you choose and, if you turn them on, reminders to drink water. They are scheduled on your phone and never say what the app is for. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing about them is sent to us.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, payment card details, browsing or search history, usage analytics or crash data. We do not ask for or use the advertising identifier.

Health information

The app keeps:

  • The bowel movement log and the symptoms, food, water, fiber and medicines you enter

This is health-related information. It is stored on your device, it is never sent to us, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with anyone.

The app does not read from or write to Apple Health.

It leaves your phone only when you share a report or file yourself. If you turn on the Face ID lock, the app asks for Face ID or your passcode before showing your log; your face data stays with iOS and is never available to the app.

The app is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. See the Terms of Use.

Who processes it

  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card details.

Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Your choices

  • Turn off notifications for the app in the Settings app.

You can delete any entry in the app, or delete every entry at once in Settings > Erase everything. Everything the app keeps is also deleted when you delete the app. A backup file you saved elsewhere (for example in Files) is yours and is not affected.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. A change that collects more data will also be described in the app's release notes.
