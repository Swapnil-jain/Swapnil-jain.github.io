Questions

Where is my log stored, and how do I move it to a new iPhone?

Your log is stored only on your iPhone. It moves to a new iPhone with an iCloud or computer backup of your phone, like other app data. You can also make a backup file in Settings > Make a backup file (part of Pro), keep it in Files or iCloud Drive, and open Settings > Restore from a backup on the new phone. Restoring is free and only adds entries; it never replaces what is already there.

What is the Bristol scale?

The Bristol Stool Form Scale sorts stool into seven types by shape and consistency, from separate hard lumps (type 1) to watery (type 7). Doctors use it so everyone means the same thing. The app shows each type with a simple drawing so you can pick the closest one.

I turned on the Face ID lock and Face ID does not work.

Tap Unlock and use your iPhone passcode instead. The lock always accepts your passcode. If your iPhone has no passcode, the lock turns itself off so you are never locked out of your own log.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Poop Tracker - Toilet Log, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Poop Tracker Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I reinstalled the app or got a new phone. How do I get Poop Tracker Pro back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings or on the Pro screen.
