GLP-1 Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-07

In short: no account, no server, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your doses, weights, side effects and everything else you log stay on your iPhone. Apple Health is used only if you turn it on, and only for your weight. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. We do not collect any data from the app.

Who we are

GLP-1 Tracker (“the app”) is made by Swapnil Jain (“we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: business@firstimpresslabs.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Your medication, dose and schedule
  • Your settings and onboarding answers
  • Your purchase status

If you make a doctor report, the PDF is built on your phone and goes only where you choose to share it.

What the app asks to use

Notifications. If you allow notifications and turn on dose reminders (part of GLP-1 Tracker Pro), the app reminds you at your dose time and, if you choose, once more 2 hours later when the dose is not logged yet. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing about them is sent to us.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, payment card details, browsing or search history, usage analytics or crash data. We do not ask for or use the advertising identifier.

Health information

The app keeps:

  • The doses you log, with the date, time, dose and injection site
  • Your weights, and your goal weight and height if you enter them
  • Side effects you log, with how strong they felt and any note
  • Protein, water and fiber you log, and your daily targets

This is health-related information. It is stored on your device, it is never sent to us, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with anyone.

The app reads or writes Apple Health data only with your permission, which you can change in the Health app.

Apple Health is optional and part of GLP-1 Tracker Pro. If you turn it on and allow it, the app saves the weights you log to Apple Health and reads weights your scale or other apps saved there. It reads and writes your weight (body mass) only. You can turn it off in the app's Settings or in the Health app. If you back up Apple Health to iCloud, that is Apple's service on your Apple Account; we cannot see it.

The app is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. See the Terms of Use.

Who processes it

  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card details.

Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Your choices

  • Turn off notifications for the app in the Settings app.

You can delete a logged dose in the app. Everything the app keeps is deleted when you delete the app. Weights the app saved to Apple Health stay in Apple Health until you delete them there.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. A change that collects more data will also be described in the app's release notes.
