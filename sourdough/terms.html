2. Predictions are estimates

Peak times and windows, “ready to use” times, bulk fermentation times and bake plan times in the app are estimates. They come from a simple fermentation model that uses your feed ratio, flour and temperature, adjusted by the peaks you log. Starters differ with flour, temperature, age and many things the app cannot see.

  • We do not guarantee that your starter or dough will behave as estimated, or that a bake will turn out as planned.
  • Reminders depend on your phone's notification settings and can be late or not shown. Watch your starter and dough, not only the clock.

3. Food safety is your responsibility

The app helps you time and measure; it does not check your food. You are responsible for food safety: use clean equipment, store your starter and dough safely, throw away a starter with mold, pink or orange streaks or an off smell, and bake bread and pizza through. Check ingredients for allergies (such as wheat and gluten) before you serve anyone. Hot ovens, baking steels and Dutch ovens can burn you.
