Sourdough Starter Tracker Log

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-14

In short: no account, no ads, no tracking. Everything you log, and the photos you add to your journal, stay on your phone. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. The app sends only anonymous usage events, with no identifier.

Who we are

Sourdough Starter Tracker Log (“the app”) is made by Swapnil Jain (“we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: business@firstimpresslabs.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Your starters, feedings and the peaks you log
  • How the app has learned your starter's pace
  • Your recipes, bake plans and settings
  • Your journal: bakes, notes, ratings and the photos you add
  • Your purchase status

A recipe card you export goes only where you choose to share it.

What the app asks to use

Photos. You can add a photo of a bake to your journal. When you pick a photo, Apple's photo picker gives the app only the photos you choose. The app cannot see the rest of your library. To save an image, the app asks only for permission to add photos to your library, which does not let it see your photos. The app keeps a copy of the photo you pick inside the app on your phone. It is not uploaded.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the app reminds you to feed your starter, tells you when it should be near its peak, and reminds you of each step of a bake plan you start. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing about them is sent to us.

What we collect

We collect anonymous usage events, such as which screens open and whether a purchase completed, to improve the app. They carry no identifier, are not linked to you, and are never used for ads or tracking.

Apart from the anonymous usage events, we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, payment card details, browsing or search history. We do not ask for or use the advertising identifier.

Who processes it

  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card details.
  • Cloudflare (our own analytics server) receives anonymous usage events, such as which screens open and whether a purchase completed, to improve the app. They carry no identifier, are not linked to you, and are never used for ads or tracking.

Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app contains no ads.

Your choices

  • Allow or remove access to your photos in the Settings app at any time.
  • Turn off notifications for the app in the Settings app.

You can delete journal entries and recipes in the app. Everything the app keeps is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. A change that collects more data will also be described in the app's release notes.
