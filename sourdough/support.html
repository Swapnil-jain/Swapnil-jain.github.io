Questions

How does the peak estimate learn my starter?

Each time you tap Fed it, the app estimates when your starter will peak from the feed ratio, the flour and your kitchen temperature. When you see your starter at its highest, tap It peaked. The app compares when it actually peaked with what it estimated and adjusts its next estimates toward your starter's pace. Each logged peak moves it only part of the way, so one unusual day does not throw it off. Your first logged peak is included free; learning from every later peak is part of Sourdough Pro.

All of this happens on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Why did my starter not peak when the app said?

The peak time is an estimate, and a starter can be faster or slower for reasons the app cannot see. Common ones:

  • The temperature was different from the one you logged. A few degrees cooler can add an hour or more.
  • A different flour, or a different amount of starter, flour or water than the ratio you picked.
  • A young starter (under about two weeks old) or one just out of the fridge, which is often slow for a feed or two.
  • The peak happened while you were not looking. A starter that has peaked starts to fall and may leave streaks on the jar above its level.

Log the time it actually peaked with It peaked, and keep the temperature up to date. The estimates get closer as you log.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Sourdough Starter Tracker Log, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Sourdough Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I reinstalled the app or got a new phone. How do I get Sourdough Pro back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings or on the Pro screen.
