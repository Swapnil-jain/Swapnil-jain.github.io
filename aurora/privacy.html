Aurora Forecast & Alerts Now

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-06

In short: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app works on your phone. Our server only hears from your phone if you turn on aurora alerts, and then it keeps the least it needs to send them: a rounded location, your alert settings, a notification token and your subscription status. Turn alerts off and that record is deleted.

Who we are

Aurora Forecast & Alerts Now (“the app”) is made by Swapnil Jain (“we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: jainswapnil138@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your location, preferences, the latest forecast and your purchase status are stored on your device and are not sent to us. The app uses your location on the device to work out darkness and your chance tonight. Location permission is optional, and approximate location is enough.

What we collect, only if you turn on alerts

Alerts are a Pro feature. When you turn them on, the app sends the following to our alert server. Nothing is sent to our server if you never turn on alerts.

DataWhat exactlyWhy
Approximate locationYour home location rounded to 0.1 degree (one decimal place, roughly 11 km), and the destinations of any trips you add, rounded the same way. The server rounds again before storing, whatever the app sends.To work out the aurora chance where you are.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, payment card details, precise location, browsing or search history, usage analytics or crash data. We do not ask for or use the advertising identifier.

This data is not linked to your identity: there is no account, and none of it tells us who you are. It is used only to provide alerts.

How long we keep it

Your alert record (location, token, purchase status, settings and trips) is deleted when any of these happens:

  • You turn alerts off in the app. It is deleted at once.
  • Your subscription ends. It is deleted after a grace period of up to 16 days, which covers Apple's billing retry so a card problem does not wipe your settings.
  • Apple's notification service tells us the token is no longer valid, for example after the app is deleted from your phone.
  • The same purchase is registered on more than 10 devices. The least recently updated one is deleted.

A lifetime purchase does not expire, so its record is kept until you turn alerts off or the token becomes invalid.

The server also keeps a record of its own scheduled checks (time and counts only, no user data) for 14 days, and a cache of cloud cover per 0.1 degree area (no user data) for up to 24 hours.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare hosts our alert server and its database, as our processor. Like any web host, it sees the IP address of each request to the server. We do not log or store IP addresses, and our server logs hold counts only, never tokens or locations.
  • Apple delivers alerts (Apple Push Notification service), handles all payments (we never see your card), and provides weather through Apple WeatherKit (“Apple Weather”). To get cloud cover, the app asks Apple Weather about your location, and our server asks about the rounded 0.1 degree locations of people with alerts on. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center supplies the aurora and Kp forecasts. The app and the server download whole global files from NOAA, so no location is sent to NOAA.

Maps use Apple MapKit, which Apple serves in real time.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Your choices

  • Use the app without location permission, or with approximate location.
  • Turn alerts off at any time to delete your server record.
  • Turn off notifications for the app in the Settings app.

Because there is no account, we cannot look up your record by name or email. Turning alerts off in the app is the way to delete it. If you can no longer do that, it is deleted as described above. You can still email us with any request and we will help where we can.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. A change that collects more data will also be described in the app's release notes.
