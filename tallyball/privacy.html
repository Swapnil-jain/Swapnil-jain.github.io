Tallyball: Pickleball Score

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-06

In short: no account, no server, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your matches, names and settings stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch, which talk to each other directly. We do not collect any data from the app.

Who we are

Tallyball: Pickleball Score (“the app”) is made by Swapnil Jain (“we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: business@firstimpresslabs.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Your matches: the one in progress and the ones you finished
  • Team and player names you type
  • Your settings and onboarding answers
  • Your purchase status

These are kept on your iPhone and, if you use one, your Apple Watch. The two send the match in progress, finished matches, settings and whether you have Tallyball Pro to each other directly, using Apple's WatchConnectivity. That goes from your iPhone to your Watch and back, never to us. Spoken score calls are made by your device's own voice and are never recorded.

What the app asks to use

Photos. You can share a picture of a match result through the iOS share sheet. The picture is made on your iPhone. To save an image, the app asks only for permission to add photos to your library, which does not let it see your photos. iOS asks for this only if you choose Save Image in the share sheet. The app never reads your photo library, and the picture goes only where you choose to send it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, payment card details, browsing or search history, usage analytics or crash data. We do not ask for or use the advertising identifier.

Health information

The app keeps:

  • A workout for each match you finish on your Apple Watch (when it started and ended, as pickleball), saved in Apple Health

This is health-related information. It is stored on your device, it is never sent to us, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with anyone.

The app reads or writes Apple Health data only with your permission, which you can change in the Health app.

This happens only if you allow it when you start your first match on the Watch. Tallyball only writes these workouts; it never reads anything from Apple Health. Running the match as a workout is what keeps the score on your Watch screen. You can turn it off at any time in the Health app or in your iPhone's Settings under Health. If you do not allow it, scoring works the same.

The app is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. See the Terms of Use.

Who processes it

  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card details.

Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Your choices

  • Allow or remove access to your photos in the Settings app at any time.

Everything the app keeps is deleted when you delete the app from your iPhone and your Apple Watch.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. A change that collects more data will also be described in the app's release notes.
