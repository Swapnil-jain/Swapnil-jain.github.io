2. The score is the one you tap

Tallyball keeps the score from the rallies you tap. It does not watch the game, so a missed or wrong tap gives a wrong score until you undo it. The serve, side, call and switch-ends reminders follow common pickleball scoring rules for the format you pick; a tournament, league or club may play by different rules.

  • The players (or the referee) decide the score, not the app. We do not guarantee that a score, server or reminder shown in the app is right for your game.
  • Keep your eyes on the ball, not your wrist, while a rally is in play.
