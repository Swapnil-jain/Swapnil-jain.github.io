Tip Calculator & Split Check

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-14

In short: No account. Your bills stay on your phone and are not sent to us; the app sends only anonymous usage events, with no identifier. The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob, which collects data from your device to show them.

Who we are

Tip Calculator & Split Check (“the app”) is made by Swapnil Jain (“we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: business@firstimpresslabs.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Your tip history (bill amount, tip, total, number of people, currency and date, last 90)
  • Your currency and settings
  • Your purchase status

What we collect

We collect anonymous usage events, such as which screens open and whether a purchase completed, to improve the app. They carry no identifier, are not linked to you, and are never used for ads or tracking.

Apart from the anonymous usage events, we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, payment card details, browsing or search history.

Ads

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show, cap and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app collects data from your device, such as its IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this data under its own policies: how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for it with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time: tap Ad privacy choices in the app's Settings (shown where consent is required).

The app asks your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before Google can use the advertising identifier. If you allow it, the ads may be personalised. If you do not, the identifier is not shared and the ads are not personalised.

Tipwise Premium removes ads, and the ads SDK is not started for Premium users.

Who processes it

  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card details.
  • Google AdMob shows ads, as described above.
  • Cloudflare (our own analytics server) receives anonymous usage events, such as which screens open and whether a purchase completed, to improve the app. They carry no identifier, are not linked to you, and are never used for ads or tracking.

Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.

Your choices

  • Change your ad consent choice: tap Ad privacy choices in the app's Settings (shown where consent is required).

You can delete history entries in the app. Everything the app keeps is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. A change that collects more data will also be described in the app's release notes.
