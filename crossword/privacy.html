Crossword Solver: Clue Helper

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-06

In short: no account, no server, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Every search runs on your phone, a word search photo is read on your phone and not kept, and we do not collect any data from the app.

Who we are

Crossword Solver: Clue Helper (“the app”) is made by Swapnil Jain (“we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: business@firstimpresslabs.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored on your device and are not sent to us:

  • The clues, letters and words you type
  • The word search grids you type or scan
  • How many free solves you have used today (kept in your phone's keychain)
  • Your settings
  • Your purchase status

The word list and dictionary are built into the app, so every search runs on your phone and works offline. On iPhones that support it, the optional “More ideas” feature asks Apple's on-device language model for guesses; it runs on your phone and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app asks to use

Camera. The app uses the camera to scan a printed word search, if you choose to. The photo is read on your phone with Apple's on-device text recognition to find the letters. The photo is not stored after the letters are read, and it is never sent to us or anyone else.

Photos. You can pick a photo of a word search so the app can read its letters on your phone. When you pick a photo, Apple's photo picker gives the app only the photos you choose. The app cannot see the rest of your library. The photo is not uploaded or stored; only the letters read from it stay in the app.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, payment card details, browsing or search history, usage analytics or crash data. We do not ask for or use the advertising identifier.

Who processes it

  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card details.
  • WordNet 3.0 from Princeton University is a dictionary built into the app under the WordNet License. Looking up a word sends nothing anywhere.
  • The ENABLE word list is a public-domain word list built into the app. Searching it sends nothing anywhere.
  • The 12dicts lists by Alan Beale are public-domain word lists built into the app. Nothing is sent.

Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Your choices

  • Allow or remove access to the camera in the Settings app at any time.

Everything the app keeps is deleted when you delete the app, except the free-solve counter in the keychain, which iOS may keep until you erase the phone. It holds only a date and a count.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. A change that collects more data will also be described in the app's release notes.
